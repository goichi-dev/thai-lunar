# Notices

The Suriyayatra reckoning in this package (`constants.go`, `year.go`,
`csdate.go`) is an independent Go implementation of the algorithm published in
[pythaidate](https://github.com/markhollow/pythaidate), MIT licensed,
Copyright (c) 2023 Mark Hollow, by way of the Dart port
[thai_lunar.dart](https://github.com/ultramcu/thai_lunar.dart).

The underlying calendar rules are documented in J. C. Eade, *Rules for
interpolation in the Thai calendar*, Journal of the Siam Society 88 (2000), and
*The Calendrical Systems of Mainland South-East Asia* (Brill, 1995).
